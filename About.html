About UniMate

Who We Are

UniMate is an educational platform designed to help university students organize their studies, access learning resources, and improve their academic performance.

Our Vision

To create a better learning experience for university students through technology and digital educational tools.

Our Mission

To make studying easier, more organized, and more effective by providing useful educational services in one place.

Our Services

  • University Courses Organization
  • Lecture Summaries
  • Study Schedule
  • Online Quizzes and Exams
  • Academic Progress Tracking
